## Scenario and sequence

An owner walks through the five places hours appear (Google profile, holiday hours, Apple Maps and Bing, their own website, Facebook/Instagram and voicemail) and fills in a table.

**Visible payoff:** A completed audit table and the holiday dates to set now.
