**Chosen line:** Your hours live in five places.

**Why it wins:** A number on the cover earns the swipe; the second line becomes the reason on slide 1.

**First frame / cover:** Daylight cover; “Your hours live / in five places.” with lime marker under “five places”; a small row of five numbered dots.

## Scenario

An owner walks through the five places hours appear (Google profile, holiday hours, Apple Maps and Bing, their own website, Facebook/Instagram and voicemail) and fills in a table.
