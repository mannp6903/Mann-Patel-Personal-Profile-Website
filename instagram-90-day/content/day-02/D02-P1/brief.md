## Opening

| | Hook | Why it could work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chosen** | The lights are on. Google says Closed. | A visible contradiction in the first two seconds; the viewer wants to know who is right. |
| Alternative | Would you drive there? | Puts the viewer in the customer’s seat, but needs the setup to land. |

**Chosen line:** The lights are on. Google says Closed.

**Why it wins:** The contradiction is the hook; “Would you drive there?” becomes the mid-film beat once the stakes are visible.

**First frame / cover:** Illustrated shop front at dusk, warm window light and a neon OPEN sign; “The lights / are on.” in large type.

## Scenario

Thursday, 6:40 PM. Example Salon started opening until 8 PM on Thursdays and updated its website, but not its Google profile, which still says it closes at 6. Sam checks on the way home, sees “Closed”, and books somewhere else.
