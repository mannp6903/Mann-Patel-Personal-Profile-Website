## Opening

| | Hook | Why it could work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chosen** | Your next customer decides in three screens. | A specific, countable promise; the viewer wants to know which three. |
| Alternative | Most customers never call you. They check you first. | Names a hidden behaviour, but is vaguer about the payoff. |

**Chosen line:** Your next customer decides in three screens.

**Why it wins:** It promises a number the film then delivers screen by screen, so every cut pays off part of the hook.

**First frame / cover:** Black frame; “Your next customer / decides in / three screens.” rises line by line, the last line in lime. Mono eyebrow names Toronto, Scarborough and North York.

## Scenario

Sam, a customer in Scarborough, looks for a deep clean on a phone. Screen 1: search results and a map. Screen 2: Sam texts the business and gets an honest automatic reply. Screen 3: Sam picks Saturday at 10:00 and sees a booking request, not a fake confirmation. Then Mann introduces himself with his real photo.
