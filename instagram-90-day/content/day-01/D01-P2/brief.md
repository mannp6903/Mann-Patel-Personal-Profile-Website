**Chosen line:** The 3-screen test.

**Why it wins:** A named test is easy to remember, send and come back to; the sub-line carries the ten-minute promise.

**First frame / cover:** Paper-white cover: “The 3-screen / test.” in heavy ink type with a lime highlighter mark on “test”, sub-line underneath and a swipe cue.

## Scenario

The owner tests their own business as a stranger would, on a phone, on mobile data, in a private window, scoring three screens: Found, Answered, Booked. A filled-in example uses a fictional cleaning company.
