04: Slide 4 of 9. A checklist with tick boxes. Text: Screen 2 of 3 Answered. Does anyone reply? Send yourself a test enquiry Through your own website form. Call from another phone after hours What happens when nobody picks up? Time the first reply Even an automatic one counts, if it’s honest.

Check what it promises Is it a request, or a confirmed time? It should say which. Look for the confirmation Did a text or email arrive?

06: Slide 6 of 9. Text: Score it Pass or fail. Nothing in between. Screen | Pass if… 1 · Found | Name, hours, phone and photos are right today 2 · Answered | Something replies, says who, and says when 3 · Booked | You can request a time and you know whether it’s confirmed Fix the first fail first. Being found doesn’t help if nobody answers.
