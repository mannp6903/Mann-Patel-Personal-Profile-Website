## Opening

| | Hook | Why it could work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chosen** | The customer thinks you got it. | Names the gap between what the customer sees and what the owner gets. |
| Alternative | Your website said “Thanks!” Your inbox said nothing. | Concrete contradiction, but long for an opening frame. |

**Chosen line:** The customer thinks you got it.

**Why it wins:** Short enough to land as a single beat after the form is sent, when the viewer has just seen the thank-you screen.

**First frame / cover:** A phone on near-black with a demo quote form; the name field is already typing; headline “Sam sends / a quote request.”

## Scenario

Sam fills in Example Cleaning Co.’s quote form and sees “Thanks! We’ll be in touch.” Priya’s inbox stays empty. The form still sends to an old address set up years ago that nobody checks.
