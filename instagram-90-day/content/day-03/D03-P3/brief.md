**Chosen line:** Every form I build admits when it can’t send.

**Why it wins:** It is specific enough to check against your own site and it is how Mann’s own site works.

**First frame / cover:** Mann’s real portrait in a rounded frame on near-black, a lime signal line, and the rule in large type beneath.

## Scenario

Mann explains a rule from his own site: if a form can’t deliver, the visitor is told and keeps what they typed, with another way to get in touch.

- Art direction: night (near-black #0B0B0C, off-white type, one lime #D4F84A accent)
- Type: Geist (display and body), Geist Mono (labels); SIL OFL 1.1
