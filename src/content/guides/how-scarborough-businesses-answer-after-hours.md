## The question

When a customer calls a salon, spa or cleaning company in Scarborough or North York after hours, what do they get back: a text, a voicemail, or nothing at all? How quickly? And can they book without waiting for a call back?

## The method

- **Who:** 30 salons, spas and cleaning companies in Scarborough and North York, from public listings on Google Maps. Write down the exact search, how the 30 were picked and how many of each kind, before the first call, so anyone could repeat it.
- **When:** one call to each, after the hours its listing shows. Write down the dates and the time window.
- **Each call:** made from a mobile, left to ring out. No message is left and no quote is asked for: the call only records what an unanswered caller gets.
- **What is logged:** a text back (and how many minutes after the call), a voicemail greeting, or nothing; and whether the business's Google profile has a booking link.
- **What is published:** totals only. No business names, numbers or anything else that could point to one business.
