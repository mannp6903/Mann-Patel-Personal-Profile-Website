## The honest answer first

- No one can make an AI assistant recommend you, or guarantee what it says. You can make accurate, public facts about your business easy to find and easy to quote.
- This work is called AEO (answer engine optimization) or GEO (generative engine optimization). In practice it is ordinary local SEO done carefully.

## Where AI answers get facts about local businesses

- Your website, your Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, Bing, directories and reviews.
- Google AI Overviews use Google Search's own index; Google says no special markup is needed for its AI features. Link: [Google: AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

## The checklist

- The same name, phone number, hours and website everywhere.
- A page for each service that answers real customer questions in plain words: what, where, how much.
- Business details and FAQs marked up with structured data.
- Reviews that describe the work, with your replies.
- Your site verified in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

## How to check what AI assistants say about you

- Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the same three questions a customer would ask, once a month.
- Log the date, the tool, the question and whether you appeared, with the facts it used.
- Answers change between tools and from day to day, so compare the log over months, not one answer.
