## What it is

- A caller who rings out gets a text from your business number, marked as automatic, saying when you'll call back or how to book.
- It is not a robocall and not an AI calling people. It only answers someone who just called you.

## Why salons and cleaners miss calls

- You're with a client or on a job when the phone rings, and the caller tries the next business.
- Link: [websites for salons and spas](/websites-for-salons-and-spas/) and [websites for cleaning companies](/websites-for-cleaning-companies/).

## What you need

- A business number that can send texts, set up and registered properly, and tested on Canadian carriers.
- A short, honest message: who you are, that the text is automatic, and what happens next (a call back, or a booking link).
- Consent records for any follow-up messages after the first reply, in line with Canada's anti-spam law.
