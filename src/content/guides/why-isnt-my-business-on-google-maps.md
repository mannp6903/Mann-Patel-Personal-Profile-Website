## Check these first

1. **No profile yet, or not verified.** Search your business name on Google Maps from a phone, signed out. Is there a profile? Is it verified?
2. **Not eligible.** Online-only businesses, and addresses you don't actually work from, aren't allowed on Maps.
3. **Suspended or duplicated.** A profile that breaks the guidelines can be suspended; two profiles for one business compete with each other.
4. **Searching from too far away.** Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence, so a search from across the city may not show you.
5. **The wrong category.** If your primary category doesn't match what people search for, you won't show up for it.
6. **A thin profile.** No photos, no hours, no services, few reviews.
7. **Details that disagree.** A different phone number or name on your website, Apple Maps, Bing or Facebook.

## What to fix, in order

- Eligibility and verification first, then category and service area, then photos, services and hours, then reviews and matching listings.
