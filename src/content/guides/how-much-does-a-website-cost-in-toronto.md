It depends on what the website has to do, and on how clearly the quote says what is included. I publish my prices, so this guide uses them as a worked example. All prices are in Canadian dollars, before tax.

## What a small business website costs with me

- **A new or redesigned website of up to five pages: $1,950.** Website Launch, with one tested enquiry form.
- **The same website, plus online booking and one follow-up workflow: $3,350.** Website + Enquiry System, for example a booking tool with reminders, or missed-call text-back.
- **One problem on your current website fixed: $450–$850,** by size. For example, a contact form that doesn't reach you, or a booking button customers can't find on a phone.
- **Your Google Business Profile and listings set up, with a review routine: $650.** Local Visibility Setup.
- **Looking after the website each month: $149 a month.** A test enquiry through your form every month and up to 30 minutes of small edits. Stop any month.

Each package has a written boundary, and anything outside it is quoted in writing before any work starts. You can [see every package side by side](/pricing/).

## What $1,950 covers

The five pages are usually Home, Services, About, Contact and one service or area page. The price includes:

- one enquiry path, such as a quote or contact form, that confirms to the customer and reaches your inbox, tested end to end
- click-to-call and one clear next step on every page
- a site built for phones first: fast to load, readable, with accessible headings, labels and contrast
- search basics: page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, and Search Console and Analytics set up in your name
- two rounds of revisions
- a handover with every login in your name, a short editing guide and the test results

## What you pay other providers

Some costs never go through me. Each provider bills you directly, at their own rates, and I list them in your written scope before you decide:

- your domain name, every year, from your registrar
- hosting, if your chosen host charges for it
- email at your domain, if you want it
- your booking tool's subscription, if it charges
- a phone number and text-message usage, if a workflow sends texts

## What changes the price of a website

- **More than five pages.** Extra pages are quoted in writing.
- **Who writes the words.** I edit the text you supply for clarity. Writing all of it from scratch is quoted separately.
- **Booking and follow-up.** Connecting a booking tool, or adding a workflow such as missed-call text-back, is the $3,350 package rather than the $1,950 one.
- **Custom features.** An instant price calculator, for example, is outside the five-page boundary.
- **Things no package includes:** online stores, photography and logo design. I can recommend people for photos and logos.

## One-time or monthly?

The website packages are one-time prices. After launch you can look after the site yourself: every login is in your name, with a short editing guide. Or you can choose Website Care at $149 a month, and stop any month.

## How to compare website quotes

Whoever you hire, ask these before you sign:

1. What exactly is included: how many pages, how many rounds of revisions, which forms? Is it written down?
2. Who owns the domain, the hosting and every login? They should be in your name.
3. Is the enquiry form tested end to end before launch, and do you get the results?
4. What isn't included, and what will it cost if you need it later?
5. What will other providers bill you each year?
6. Does anyone promise you a position on Google? Google decides rankings, so no one can promise one.
