## The situation

A new cleaning business starts with no reviews, no name recognition and a crowded local market. People choosing a cleaner on their phone want three answers quickly: **what will it cost, do you come to my area, and can I trust the person coming into my home?** Nest & Rest had to answer all three before anyone had heard of it.

## Who it's for

Busy households in Scarborough and North York, in condos, townhouses and houses, who mostly arrive on a phone and decide within a minute.

## Scope

- An offer of six services: four with instant prices and two by quote (larger homes and small offices)
- The website: home page, six service pages, two area pages, a booking-request flow, a vision page, service policies, guides and FAQ
- Ways to get in touch: online booking request, call, text and WhatsApp
- A written visit standard, "five things happen at your door"

## Decisions that shaped it

1. **Price first.** The home page opens with an estimate built from the service, type of home, bedrooms and bathrooms, rather than a "get a quote" form. Not knowing the price is a common reason to leave a cleaning site without asking.
2. **Specific trust, not adjectives.** Instead of "trusted professionals", the site lists what happens at every door, such as indoor-only shoes and separate aprons for kitchens and bathrooms, with simple illustrations.
3. **Two districts, not "the whole GTA".** Separate pages for Scarborough and North York neighbourhoods, plus a postal-code check, make the promise of being on time believable.
4. **Honest booking.** A booking is a *request*, confirmed personally by text. Payment happens after the clean, with no deposit.
5. **Thumb-friendly on mobile.** A fixed bar keeps Call, Text and Book within reach on every page.

## Results

None are claimed here. The business launched in October 2026, and it's too early for honest numbers. Prices also changed between the two archived versions of the site, a reminder to keep pricing in one place so it can change safely.

## What this shows for other owners

The same foundations apply to most local service businesses: a clear offer and price signal, a believable service area, specific promises, and an enquiry path that says honestly what happens next.
