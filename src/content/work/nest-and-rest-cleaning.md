## The situation

A new cleaning business starts with no reviews, no name recognition and a crowded local market. People choosing a cleaner on their phone want three answers quickly: **what will it cost, do you come to my area, and can I trust the person coming into my home?** Nest & Rest had to answer all three before anyone had heard of it.

## Who it's for

Busy households in Scarborough and North York, in condos, townhouses and houses, plus Airbnb hosts who need same-day turnovers. Most arrive on a phone.

## Scope

- Six home services with prices shown up front, plus Airbnb turnovers, residence care, small offices and a page for realtors
- The website: home page, service pages, pricing page, a room-by-room checklist, area and neighbourhood pages, a booking-request flow, gift cards, guides, a vision page and service policies
- Ways to get in touch: booking request, email, and a "text me this price" option
- A written visit standard: five things that happen at every visit

## Decisions that shaped it

1. **Price first.** The home page opens with an estimate built from the service, home type, bedrooms and bathrooms, rather than a "get a quote" form.
2. **Show exactly what's included.** A room-by-room explorer lists what each service covers, what costs extra and what isn't part of the clean, so there are no surprises.
3. **The owner is the brand.** "Hi, I'm Mann. I clean every home myself." The person at the door will be the person who runs the business, and the site says so.
4. **Believable areas.** Two districts, with neighbourhood pages and a postal-code check, instead of claiming the whole GTA.
5. **Honest booking.** Sending the form is a request; Mann confirms the time and final price personally. Payment happens after the clean, with no deposit.

## How it evolved

The archived 19 October version had the estimator, services and the visit standard. By 31 October the site had added Airbnb turnovers, gift cards, the room-by-room checklist, a pricing page, guides and neighbourhood pages. Small, real improvements, shipped quickly.

## Results

None are claimed here. The website went live in October 2026, and the business starts taking clients in summer 2027.
