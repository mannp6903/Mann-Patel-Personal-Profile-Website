## The brief (fictional)

Kettle & Crumb is an imagined two-person bakery-café opening on a Toronto high street. The owners need to look established from day one: a sign-ready logo, cards for suppliers, a flyer for the neighbourhood, an opening post, gift cards for the counter, and a simple website that answers "what's on, when are you open, where are you".

## The approach

- **One idea, everywhere.** A warm, bookish serif and a kettle mark with rising steam, in crust brown on flour cream with a jam-red accent. Every piece uses the same three colours, so the card, flyer and post feel like one place.
- **Information first.** Opening hours and address appear on the flyer, the post and the website hero in the same order. Customers shouldn't have to hunt.
- **Gift-card artwork only.** The card design is artwork. Issuing, selling, tracking and redeeming gift cards needs a separate point-of-sale or gift-card system, and the sample says so.

## What a real project would add

Photographs of the real food and space (never stock photos presented as the business's own), the owners' real hours, prices and menu, a Business Profile check, and print files prepared to the chosen printer's specifications.
