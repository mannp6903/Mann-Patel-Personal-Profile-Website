## Executive summary

**Verdict: strong foundation, almost no reach yet.** The site is technically excellent (mobile PageSpeed 98 performance, 100 accessibility, 100 best practices, 100 SEO). But I could not find mannpatel.me in search results for its domain or your name. It has zero reviews, and no other site links to it.

**Biggest strength:** the build. Every page has a unique title, a canonical tag and one H1, and 11 of 14 carry structured data. The homepage paints in 1.0 s on a throttled phone, about 5x faster than The Ant Firm, the closest local competitor (4.9 s). Published prices and honest case studies are rare in this market.

**Top 3 priorities:**

1. **Get found at all.** Verify the site in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, submit the sitemap and request indexing. Finish your Google Business Profile as a service-area business.
2. **Earn the first trust signals.** Get 2–3 real Google reviews, add a "Website by Mann Patel" credit link on nestnrestcleaning.com, and list the business on 6–8 directories with identical details.
3. **Build pages for searches you can actually win.** A Scarborough page, two industry pages (cleaning, salons) backed by your case studies, and a guides section. Also cut the /services/ hub, which repeats 72% of /pricing/.

One extra risk: "Mann Patel" is shared by many developers online, and searches for the name return their portfolios. Pair your name with "Toronto web designer" everywhere (GBP, LinkedIn, Facebook, schema) so search engines and AI tools can tell you apart.

## Prioritized action plan

Do the eight quick wins first: together they take 4–5 hours and unblock everything else.

### Quick wins (this week)

- [ ] **Verify mannpatel.me in Google Search Console** Submit sitemap.xml, then request indexing for /, /services/web-design/, /services/local-visibility/, /services/enquiry-follow-up/, /pricing/ and /about/. Impact high · 30 min · no dependency.
- [ ] **Verify the site in Bing Webmaster Tools** Import from Search Console in one click. Bing's index powers Copilot and is one of the sources ChatGPT search draws on. Impact high · 15 min · after Search Console.
- [ ] **Finish your Google Business Profile** Service-area business with the home address hidden, primary category "Website designer", areas Scarborough, North York and Toronto, services with prices, photos, and a link to mannpatel.me. Impact high · 1–2 hrs · needs Google verification.
- [ ] **Add a "Website by Mann Patel" footer link on nestnrestcleaning.com** It doesn't link to you today. Ask Samp Hair & Spa for the same credit at launch. Impact medium · 15 min.
- [ ] **Add a business entity to the schema** Add one ProfessionalService block (name, url, telephone +1-647-978-6903, email, areaServed, priceRange, founder pointing to your Person). Add your Facebook Page and, once live, the GBP link to sameAs. Drop worksFor "Nest & Rest Cleaning": it tells Google you work for a cleaning company. Impact high · 1 hr.
- [ ] **Shorten two meta descriptions** Home is 172 characters and /pricing/ is 213; Google cuts at about 155. Suggested copy is in the on-page table. Impact medium · 15 min.
- [ ] **Put "Toronto" in the homepage H1 and on /pricing/** The H1 names no place or customer, and /pricing/ never mentions Toronto. Impact medium · 15 min.
- [ ] **Add lastmod dates to the sitemap** Astro's sitemap integration can set them from build or file dates. Impact low · 15 min.

### Strategic investments (this quarter)

- [ ] **Slim the /services/ hub to an overview** It is 6,641 words and repeats 72% of /pricing/ and 43% of /services/web-design/. Cut it to about 600 words that route to each service page; keep package detail on /pricing/ only. Impact high · half day.
- [ ] **Build a Scarborough page** You're based there and Samp Hair & Spa is there. Use real local proof, neighbourhoods served, the GBP link and an FAQ. Add North York later only when you have proof there too. Impact high · half day · GBP live first.
- [ ] **Build two industry pages: cleaning companies and salons/spas** Each one leads with its case study, then the package that fits. Impact high · 1 day each.
- [ ] **Start a /guides/ section with four posts, one every two weeks** Topics are in Content gaps. Each ends with the free check offer. Impact high · 3–4 hrs per post.
- [ ] **Set up a review routine** Ask every client at handover, reply to every review, and show them on the homepage. Never buy or swap reviews (your own rule). Impact high · ongoing · needs GBP.
- [ ] **List the business on 6–8 directories with identical name, phone and URL** Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Clutch, DesignRush, GoodFirms and Digital Main Street's vendor list. Impact medium · 2–3 hrs.
- [ ] **Check AI answers once a month** Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini "web designer in Scarborough for a small business" and two other buyer questions; log whether you appear. Impact medium · 30 min a month.
