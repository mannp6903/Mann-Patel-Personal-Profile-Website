## Brand & Content Sprint price (OFFER-2)

Mann asked for the Sprint to be priced from competitor research (23 Nov 2026). The audit's five competitors
(Adrian Martinez, BridgeWeb, WebZylo, We Are Digital Marketing, Taylor Nguyen) publish no price for social content or
brand kits (BridgeWeb and WebZylo publish no prices at all; We Are Digital Marketing's site couldn't be reached from the
build machine), so the comparison uses Toronto and Canadian providers that do publish one.

**What the Sprint is:** one short campaign from one service story: a one-page style and messaging sheet from the client's
approved logo, colours and photos, three captioned post designs, two Reel hooks with scripts to film on a phone, one round
of changes and every file handed over. No filming, editing, posting or new logo.

| Benchmark | Published price | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-time social profile design with a template set, one platform (Thrive Solutions, Toronto) | from $600 CAD + HST | thrive-solutions.net/services/social-media-marketing/social-profile-design |
| One-time brand kit: logos, social icons, palette, style guide (Jenny Henderson Studio, Toronto) | $800 CAD | jennyhendersonstudio.com/brand-kit-3 |
| Single post designs (Canadian price guides) | $50–$250 a post | ap-digital.ca (2026 guide); ltlcreative.ca (Calgary, $40–$150) |
| Static post / carousel design (2026 guide) | $75–$250 / $150–$450 | 12amagency.com |
| One-page brand style sheet | "a few hundred dollars" | kapa99.com; Fiverr cost guide |
| Monthly posting plans, for scale | $199/mo (8 posts, Casa Media House); $500/mo (30 posts + 4 Reels, GTA Social Media) | casamediahouse.com; gtasocialmedia.com |
| Filmed Reels (not part of the Sprint) | $700–$1,500 a shoot | torontovipmarketing.com; shout-media.ca |

The parts add up to about $450–$1,200 (a sheet of a few hundred dollars, three posts at $75–$250, two short scripts).

**Price set: $550 CAD plus tax, one-time.** It sits below the two Toronto one-time kits ($600 and $800), inside the
per-piece market, and with Mann's other prices (each ends in 50). At the hourly rate the $1,950 website implies, it is
about 7 hours of work. **To validate:** Mann's real hours for one Sprint, and the timeline wording in
`src/data/packages.ts`.

The price lives in `src/data/packages.ts` (package `content-sprint`), so /pricing/, /services/, the brief form,
the structured data and `public/llms.txt` show the same figure.
